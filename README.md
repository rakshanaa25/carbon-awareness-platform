# 🌱 EcoTrace – Carbon Footprint Awareness Platform

## Chosen Vertical

**Sustainability & Climate Awareness**

EcoTrace addresses the challenge of helping individuals understand, track, and reduce their carbon footprint through education, personalized assessment, and actionable recommendations.

---

## Problem Statement

Design a solution that helps individuals understand, track, and reduce their carbon footprint through simple actions and personalized insights.

Many individuals want to contribute towards sustainability but lack visibility into how their daily activities impact the environment. Carbon emissions often feel abstract and difficult to measure, making it challenging to identify meaningful lifestyle changes.

---

## Approach and Logic

The solution was designed with three primary objectives:

1. **Educate** users about carbon footprints and the major factors contributing to emissions.
2. **Assess** individual environmental impact through a simple questionnaire covering transportation, energy consumption, food habits, consumer goods, and waste generation.
3. **Guide** users toward sustainable choices through personalized recommendations and weekly sustainability challenges.

The platform combines educational content with interactive assessment and visual analytics to make environmental awareness more engaging and actionable.

---

## How the Solution Works

### 1. Awareness & Learning

Users can explore educational sections explaining:

* What a carbon footprint is
* Major emission drivers
* The environmental impact of everyday activities
* Practical sustainability concepts

### 2. Carbon Footprint Assessment

Users complete a questionnaire about their:

* Transportation habits
* Household energy usage
* Dietary preferences
* Consumer purchasing behavior
* Waste management practices

### 3. Carbon Impact Calculation

The platform processes the inputs and estimates the user's annual carbon footprint using predefined emission factors and calculation logic.

### 4. Personalized Insights

Based on the assessment:

* A carbon score is generated
* Major contributors are identified
* An AI-generated sustainability narrative is displayed
* Users receive personalized reduction recommendations

### 5. Data Visualization

Results are presented through:

* Progress indicators
* Interactive charts
* Emission breakdowns
* Impact summaries

### 6. Sustainability Challenges

Users can explore weekly sustainability challenges designed to encourage long-term environmentally responsible habits.

## Assumptions Made

* The platform uses generalized emission factors for estimation purposes.
* User-provided information is assumed to be accurate.
* Results are intended for awareness and educational purposes rather than scientific auditing.
* AI-generated narratives are simulated using predefined prompt templates and do not rely on external climate databases.
* Emission estimates represent approximate annual impact and may vary from real-world values.
